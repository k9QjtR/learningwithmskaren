What to expect

Come ready to sing, move, laugh and play.

Each class is designed to feel relaxed and welcoming. Children can join activities at their own pace while caregivers model participation and enjoy the experience too.

✓

Interactive. Children learn by doing rather than sitting still.

✓

Developmentally appropriate. Activities meet children where they are.

✓

No pressure. Observing, warming up slowly and repeating favorites are all part of learning.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What ages are classes for?

Learning with Ms. Karen focuses on children from birth through age 5, with caregiver participation.

Does a caregiver stay during class?

Yes. These are caregiver-and-child experiences, so grown-ups participate alongside their child.

Where are classes held?

Classes take place at various locations throughout Middletown, Odessa and Townsend. Exact locations may vary by session.

Do we need to bring anything?

Comfortable clothes and a willingness to play are the essentials. Any class-specific items can be shared before the session.
